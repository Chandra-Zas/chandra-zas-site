Who is Chandra Zas

I'm a small-town girl who went searching the world for the feeling of home within my own bones. I grew up in the Sierra foothills, and in my early twenties I set out to travel, chasing that sense of home inside my body, my heart, my being.

That search became my life's work. I'm wired to hold big, hard emotions with compassion and acceptance, and I've spent thousands of hours in therapy, in retreats and workshops, and in my own healing, learning to stay steady through the roller coaster of personal and collective transformation. It's what lets me zoom out, see the pattern beneath the struggle, and ask the honest, open questions that lead you home to your own inner wisdom and body-knowing.

My searching led me here, to the work I love most: helping people heal, grow, and transform.

I'm most complimented for my parenting. And… I'm far from perfect. I have plenty of my own struggles that I'm still healing and growing through.

For the kids, and you. And our collective human growth.

My Mission

I believe we are at a pivotal moment in human consciousness. Every one of us who can help move the needle, for our own growth and for our collective evolution, matters now more than ever. That is the difference I am here to make.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Chandra Zas?

Chandra Zas is a somatic coach, author, and the Book Momma of the Handbook for Human Potential. She helps people heal, grow, and transform, moving from inherited patterns to presence through somatic coaching, the Mood Before Food method, and nervous-system work. Based in Reno, Nevada, she works with clients through Zen Odyssey.

What is a somatic coach?

A somatic coach helps you work with the body, not only the mind. Rather than trying to think your way out of stress, cravings, or reactive patterns, somatic coaching helps you regulate your nervous system and listen to your body's signals, so lasting change becomes possible.

How can I work with Chandra Zas?

You can book a call with Chandra and explore her coaching, courses, and the Zen Odyssey Studio at zenodyssey.com. You can also read her work in the Handbook for Human Potential.
